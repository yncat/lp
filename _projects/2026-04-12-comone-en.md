In 2026, as Audio Game Center, I served as supervisor and facilitator for the [ComoNe Program #02 [Audio Game Center + ComoNe] Hackathon "Let's Make Games from Sound!"](https://artscape.jp/exhibitions/64694/).

This time, I didn't double as an engineer and simply took part as a facilitator. Along the way, I also made [a little game](https://nyanchangames.com/nagoya-hackathon/app/) of my own and introduced it.

Partly because coding with AI agents has become widespread, this hackathon didn't feel like engineers writing tons of code. My impression was that the works came together through repeated prototyping.

The participants included many creative members, and lots of unique works were born. The works are on display within the "ComoNe Program #02 Hello Human!" special exhibition until September 2026.
